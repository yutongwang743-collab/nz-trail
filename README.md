# NZ Trail

AI-powered travel planning platform for people who want to explore New Zealand without spending hours planning.

---

# Vision

NZ Trail is designed for travelers who:

- Don't know how to plan a trip
- Feel overwhelmed by too many travel options
- Want fast and reliable travel suggestions
- Prefer guided itineraries instead of researching everything manually

The goal is to reduce travel planning stress and help users quickly discover the best routes, activities, and recommendations.

---

# Product Positioning

NZ Trail is not a traditional booking website.

Instead of focusing on:
- hotel booking
- flight comparison
- complex search systems

NZ Trail focuses on:
- travel guidance
- itinerary generation
- recommendation systems
- simplified decision making

Core idea:

> "Stop planning. Start exploring."

# Target Users

## Primary Users

### First-time Travelers
People who have never visited New Zealand before and don't know where to start.

### Students & Budget Travelers
Users who want affordable and optimized travel plans.

### Busy People
People who don't want to spend hours researching routes and activities.

### Couples & Small Groups
Travelers looking for easy-to-follow travel experiences.

---

# Core Problems To Solve

## Problem 1 — Too Many Choices
Most travel websites overload users with information.

Users often:
- open too many tabs
- compare endlessly
- become mentally exhausted

NZ Trail simplifies this process.

---

## Problem 2 — Planning Takes Too Long
Users don't want to manually:
- build routes
- calculate budgets
- organize activities
- search transportation

The platform should generate suggestions quickly.

---

## Problem 3 — Lack of Confidence
Many users are unsure:
- which places are worth visiting
- how many days are enough
- what route makes sense

NZ Trail acts like a travel assistant.

# Design Principles

## 1. Reduce Decision Fatigue

Every page should help users make decisions faster.

Avoid:
- too many filters
- cluttered layouts
- overwhelming information

Prioritize:
- curated recommendations
- guided choices
- clear actions

---

## 2. Make Travel Feel Easy

Users should feel:
- relaxed
- excited
- guided

The experience should feel like:
> "Someone already planned this for me."

---

## 3. Prioritize Routes Over Locations

Users care more about:
- "How should I travel?"
than:
- "What places exist?"

Focus on:
- itineraries
- travel flows
- suggested schedules

instead of simple destination cards.
